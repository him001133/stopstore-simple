    <h2>Why Choose LED Wall Lights?</h2>
    <p>According to the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), switching to LED technology can reduce household energy consumption significantly. Here is why they are perfect for Indian homes:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Cost-Effective:</strong> Massive savings on electricity bills.</li>
        <li><strong>Longevity:</strong> Most LEDs last between 20,000 to 50,000 hours.</li>
        <li><strong>Low Heat Emission:</strong> Perfect for the Indian climate.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Buying Guide: What to Look For (Under ₹500)</h2>
    <h3>1. IP Rating (Ingress Protection)</h3>
    <p>If you are buying lights for balconies or exteriors, look for an <strong>IP65 rating</strong>. This ensures protection against rain during the Indian monsoon.</p>

    <h3>2. Color Temperature (CCT)</h3>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Warm White (2700K - 3000K):</strong> Best for bedrooms and living rooms.</li>
        <li><strong>Cool White (5000K - 6500K):</strong> Ideal for kitchens or hallways.</li>
    </ul>
